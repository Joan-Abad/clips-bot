Privacy Policy – Clips Bot

Last updated: 6 October 2026

Clips Bot is a personal, non-commercial tool used only by its owner to publish daily gaming highlight videos to the owner's own channels: Yesterday in RP (GTA roleplay highlights) and Dios del Fennec (Rocket League highlights), on YouTube and TikTok.

What data the app accesses

  • OAuth access tokens for the owner's own YouTube and TikTok accounts, used only to upload videos (or drafts), set thumbnails and manage playlists on those accounts.
  • Basic profile information of the connected account (such as its display name), only to confirm which account is connected.
  • Public information about publicly available Twitch/Kick clips (title, channel name, view count), used to choose and credit the clips.

What the app does not do

  • It does not collect, sell or share personal data of viewers or third parties.
  • It does not use cookies, analytics or advertising trackers.
  • It is not offered to other users; only the owner's own accounts are connected.

Storage and security

Tokens are stored only on the owner's private computer and are never shared. The owner can revoke access at any time from the YouTube (Google account) or TikTok security settings, which makes the stored tokens useless.
